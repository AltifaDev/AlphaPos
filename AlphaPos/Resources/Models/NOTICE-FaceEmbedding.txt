FaceEmbedding.mlpackage

This model is based on face-reidentification-retail-0095 from the Intel Open Model Zoo.
The model and the accompanying conversion artifacts are distributed under the Apache
License, Version 2.0. The upstream model produces a 256-dimensional face embedding
from a tightly aligned 128x128 BGR face crop. AlphaPos converts the input to RGB and
uses cosine similarity for verification.

Upstream documentation:
https://github.com/openvinotoolkit/open_model_zoo/tree/master/models/intel/face-reidentification-retail-0095

Apache License 2.0:
https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0

The model is not an Apple Face ID or Touch ID component. Apple biometric templates
remain protected by the Secure Enclave and are never read or stored by AlphaPos.
